Know someone who is selling their car or looking for one? Recommend us. When the deal closes, we pay you your commission.

## How it works

1. **Send us the contact:** their name, their WhatsApp and which car they're selling or looking for. You can use the form on the home page (**Referrals** section) or message us directly.
2. **Let your referral know** that we're going to message them and that they come from you.
3. **We take care of them** and tell them you sent them.
4. **The deal closes and we pay you your commission.**

Selling or buying: both count. We only pay on closed deals.

## How much is the commission?

We'll confirm the amount via WhatsApp before closing the deal.

## Refer now

Message us on WhatsApp with the person's name and number and which car they're selling or looking for. We'll take care of the rest.
