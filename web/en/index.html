How we buy a car

And why we turn down most of them.

STEP 1

Papers

VIN, ATTT, clearance certificate and liens. If something doesn't add up, we stop.

STEP 2

Flood check

Carpets, seat rails, connectors, smell. In Panama it's a must.

STEP 3

Scanner and shop

Electronic diagnostics and a lift inspection before paying a single dollar.

STEP 4

Transparency

We show you the flaws, the repairs and the history. No surprises.
